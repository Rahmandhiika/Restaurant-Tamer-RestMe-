## Objective
Drag from a dispenser spawns a movable copy; the dispenser itself is never consumed. Dropping bun/isian/raw meat in the serve area before plate is there = invalid (delete the dropped item, no snap-back — same rule for every drag stage in this project, see docs/PRD.md §4.2 and §5 Day 2 log). Plate dropped first is valid and becomes the "alas". Order of bun/isian/meat after that is free.
